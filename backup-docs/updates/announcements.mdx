<Update label="25/11/2024" description="Progressive Discounts on Payment Slips">
  
  Hello, partner!

With the aim of improving our processes and always bringing new features to enhance your operation, starting next Thursday, November 28th, you will be able to register payment slips with progressive discounts for up to 3 different dates.

How to join?

To adopt the progressive discount, it will be necessary to fill in the complementary line of type 3 of the CNAB 400.
