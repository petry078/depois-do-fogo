This modality allows annual withdrawals of FGTS in the month of the borrower's birthday. 

It is important to note that joining the Anniversary Withdrawal is optional and that customers who do not opt for this model will remain in the standard Termination Withdrawal modality.

## Differences between the modalities

* **Withdrawal Termination:** when the employee is dismissed without just cause, they can withdraw the full balance of the FGTS account, including the termination fine, when due. This is the standard modality in which the employee joins the FGTS;
* **Withdrawal Anniversary:** this is an optional benefit in which the employee can withdraw part of the amount they has in the FGTS account, once a year, in the month of his/her birthday. In the case of dismissal without just cause, the employee may withdraw only the amount corresponding to the termination fine.
