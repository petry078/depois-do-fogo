---
title: "13 - Certifier"
openapi: post /Proposta/IncluirDocumento
---

Through this endpoint, the partner executes the signature of the proposal via a Certifier. Examples of Certifiers: Docusign, QCertifica, Clicksign, Finanblue, among others.
The signature process begins when the partner downloads the CCB without the MODELO watermark found on our portal, then signs it via a Certifier and then attaches the CCB to the proposal again.
