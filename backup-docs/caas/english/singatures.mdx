## Complete Guide to the Signature Methods of Bank Credit Notes

The method of signing Bank Credit Notes (CCBs) is fundamental to ensure the legal validity and security of credit operations. The way a CCB is signed directly impacts its authenticity and protection against fraud.

Choosing the appropriate method guarantees the validity of the CCB, protects the parties involved, and provides more agility in any judicial collections. Therefore, adopting secure signing practices is essential for the credibility and efficiency of the credit granting process.

This guide has been prepared to offer a comprehensive and practical view of the different methods of signing CCBs. We will present the types of signatures accepted in the market, the advantages of each method, as well as guidance to ensure legal compliance and the security of operations.

## [1. Electronic (BMP Digital)](https://bmpdocs.moneyp.com.br/caas/api-reference/assinaturas/eletronica)

### What is it?

The Electronic Signature is a method that uses tokens sent via SMS or WhatsApp, providing an innovative approach to electronic authentication. This method uses a unique security code sent directly to the user’s mobile device, ensuring precise identity validation and protection against fraud.

With direct integration to common mobile devices, signing documents becomes a simple and straightforward process. The fast and reliable delivery of tokens via SMS or WhatsApp contributes to an agile signing process, facilitating important transactions with ease and security.

### How to Use?

1) First, use the API to inform the person responsible for signing the proposal, along with the customer's contact information (cell phone number and email) to receive the signature link.

2) After opening the link, creating an access password is necessary.

3) Next, the signer must request the sending of the signature token.

4) The signature is generated by filling in the token.

The change of the proposal status from **“approved”** to **“finalized”** is carried out automatically by the system after all signers complete the signing process, and after that, we will make the payment release request for the proposal available.

## [2. Relogon Signature](https://bmpdocs.moneyp.com.br/caas/api-reference/assinaturas/relogon)

### What is it?

The Relogon Signature is a method of authentication and electronic signing, specially designed for self-contracting or end-to-end contracting flows directly on the platform, providing an integrated and continuous experience for the client. With this method, the user can complete the entire credit journey without needing to leave the platform, maintaining a smooth and efficient experience. Security is enhanced through dual authentication before completing the signature, ensuring an extra layer of protection and trust in the process.

### How to Use?

For the signature to be validated, the following requirements must be met within your application:

1) **Login and validation:** The partner must be logged in and validated in a secure environment.

2) **Mandatory check-box:** The partner must check the box with the following statement:  

This instrument is generated and electronically signed, ensuring its authenticity, integrity, and validity according to the law. The signer hereby ratifies that they accept as valid, for the purposes of proving authorship and integrity, the signature and information contained in this document, which were captured electronically, constituting an extrajudicial enforceable title according to the law and for all legal purposes, within the ICP BRAZIL standards for "tracing the digital audit trail" (chain of custody) of the document, in order to verify its integrity and authenticity, as stated by art. 10 of Provisional Measure No. 2 200/2001.

3) **Viewing and availability of the CCB:** The client needs to access and review the CCB model before proceeding with the signature. Before signing, we will provide a link for the client to view the watermarked version of the CCB. After signing, this same link will provide the final version, containing all responsible signatures.

4) **Signature capture:** At the moment of signing, the partner must capture the IP and date/time data of the signers.

5) **Sending data via API:** After the process, the partner should send the obtained data via API to evidence that the signature was successfully completed.

6) **Call to finalize the process:** After sending all signatures, it will be necessary to make an API call to finalize the proposal, indicating that the proposal has been signed by the parties. The endpoint for this submission is the following:  

https://bmpdocs.moneyp.com.br/caas/api-reference/assinaturas/relogon

### What are the Particularities?

The characteristics of the Relogon Signature require that the integration partner performs the authentication of the final client within their own process flow. Additionally, the partner must validate, through this method, the exact moment when the signature was captured on their platform. With this, BMP can generate an electronic HASH linked to the signature, which will then be attached to the Bank Credit Note (CCB), ensuring the integrity and authenticity of the document.

### When to Use?

The Relogon Signature is indicated for situations where the client is already authenticated on a portal, contracting kiosks, or any other end-to-end journey within an application, where the partner already uses a final client authentication method. This method is especially advantageous when the partner only wishes to confirm the signature with BMP to generate the electronic HASH, ensuring a continuous and secure user experience without redirects or interruptions in the journey.

### What are the advantages of the Relogon Signature:

- **Integrated Experience:** Allows the client to finalize the signing process within the partner's own environment, without needing to leave the platform or be redirected.

- **Efficiency:** The capture and confirmation of the signature occur quickly and efficiently, optimizing the partner's workflow and ensuring a smooth experience for the client.

- **Flexibility:** Can be used in various applications and environments, such as web portals, contracting kiosks, or any system that the partner uses for interaction with the final client.

## [3. Digital (Signature House)](https://bmpdocs.moneyp.com.br/caas/api-reference/assinaturas/certificadora)

### What is it?

The method of signing via certification houses is a modern and secure way to digitally authenticate documents. These platforms offer electronic signing solutions that allow users to sign documents remotely, with legal validity and regulatory compliance.

The digital signature uses encryption to authenticate the signer's identity and ensure the integrity of the document, linked to a digital certificate issued by a Certification Authority (CA).

The digitally signed document can be stored and shared electronically.

In digital signing, the proposal is signed using services such as Docusign, QCertifica, Clicksign, Finanblue, among others (at the partner's choice).

The signing methods available are chosen by the integration partner, and all responsibility for security, assurance, and compliance rests with the selected external signing house. This entity has no systemic link or direct integration with BMP. In this process, BMP merely provides the bank credit note (CCB) and the endpoints for sending the signed document.

### How Does It Work?

1) First, the partner downloads the model CCB (without the watermark) through a printing URL.

2) With the model CCB in hand, the partner carries out the signing process through the chosen certifier.

3) To complete the procedure, when the CCB is signed, it must be reattached to the proposal via API, sending the document in **Base64**.

Endpoints for sending the signed CCB:

https://bmpdocs.moneyp.com.br/caas/api-reference/assinaturas/certificadora

### When to Use?

This method is generally used when the integration partner already has a contract with a digital signature company and needs to use this solution to digitally sign all their contracts.

### Advantages of Digital Signature for CCBs:

- **Security:** Ensures the authenticity and integrity of the document, preventing fraud.

- **Legal Validity:** Has the same legal validity as physical signatures, provided they meet the established legal requirements.

- **Traceability:** Allows a record of who signed and when it occurred, helping to maintain a history of the processes carried out.

## [4. Cartular](https://bmpdocs.moneyp.com.br/caas/api-reference/assinaturas/certificadora)

### What is it?

The "cartular signature of CCB involves the use of a certifier to validate a physical document related to a Bank Credit Note (CCB).

This involves signing a paper document, that is, the proposal. "Cartular" means that the document is in physical format, unlike electronic documents.

A certifier is involved to validate the signatures on a physical document of CCB. This may include verifying the identity of the individuals who signed the document and ensuring that the signature is trustworthy.

The certifier will provide a statement confirming that the CCB document was signed according to legal requirements.

### How Does It Work?

1) The partner must download the model version of the CCB (without the watermark) through the printing URL.

2) With the model version of the CCB, it must be signed by the certifier of the partner's choice.

3) To complete the procedure, when the CCB is signed, it must be reattached to the proposal via API, sending the document in **Base64**.

### When to Use?

The cartular signature is generally applied in cases with a smaller volume of proposals or in situations requiring notarization, as well as other more specific scenarios.
