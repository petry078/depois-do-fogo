This introduction is designed to ensure that all aspects of the system are functional and secure before entering the production environment. The following steps must be completed to ensure the integrity and operability of BMP solutions.

<Warning>
This checklist is mandatory for entry into production.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Communication Channel">
    As our partner, you will be added to a communication channel to facilitate interaction among all parties involved in the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Project Scope">
    There will be a scope alignment for the project, and the schedule will be presented.

     <Info>At this stage, rely on our team for the presentation of our APIs and our cases for a better definition of your project.</Info>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Public Key Registration">
    At this stage, the public key will be registered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery of Homologation Credentials">
    At this stage, our team will deliver the credentials for the homologation environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="JWT and Token Creation">
    Implement JWTs and access tokens for authentication and authorization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="External Development">
    The partner must integrate with our APIs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proposal Generation by Environment">
    It will be necessary to send 3 proposals, namely:
    - Proposal in finalized status with incorrect disbursement banking details
    - Proposals in finalized status with correct disbursement banking details
    - Proposal in released status with correct disbursement banking details
    <Warning> In cases where the calculation is issued by the partner, send the calculation memory for analysis by the implementation team. </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Internal BMP Homologation">
    Conduct internal homologation tests to validate integrations and functionalities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signoff">
    Obtain final approval from all involved BackOffice areas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Production Credentialing">
    Carry out the necessary procedures to prepare and approve the system for production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery of Production Environment">
    Transfer the system to the production environment and begin operations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Planning 

- Communication will be conducted through the project group via WhatsApp.
- Our business hours are from 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday.
- We do not release Production Credentials on Fridays and the day before holidays.
- Practice 1 request per second (Rate Limit in Production).
