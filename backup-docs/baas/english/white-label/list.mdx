### Next Steps:

#### Signing and Terms

- **Commercial Contact:** Contact our sales team to sign the contract and terms of service.

#### Infrastructure

- **Dedicated Fixed IP:** The IP must be dedicated to the network used by operators to access the backend. Without this registered IP, platforms cannot be released.

#### Onboarding

- **Technical Onboarding:** Conduct onboarding with BMP's technical team to align timelines and expectations.
- **Material Submission:** Send requested materials to the team, including logo, colors, and checklist.
- **Screen Approval:** Approval of screens created from logo and color submissions. The SLA for screen creation is up to 2 business days after submission. Any modifications reset the timeline.

#### App Publishing

- **Store Contracting:** It's the partner's responsibility to contract with Apple and Google stores. BMP only assists with publication after this contracting. Accounts must be legal organizational accounts.
- **DUNS:** Request a DUNS number, necessary for use in the Apple store ([Learn more](link)).
- **Developer Inclusion:** Include BMP's developer with permission to publish apps. The publication timeframe can vary from 7 to 20 business days, depending on the stores.

#### Websites and Training

- **Institutional Website and Privacy Policy:** Prepare your institutional website and privacy policy page.
- **Backend Training:** After Apps are released, conduct backend training with BMP's technical team, lasting between 1 to 1.5 hours.

#### Production Entry

- **Final Release:** Production entry will occur after platform release and training completion.

### Partner Entry Checklist

#### For WEB

- Partner's institutional website URL.
- Email.
- Internal contact option: phone, email, or contact link (choose only one).
- Security email.
- CNPJ (Brazilian company registration number).

#### For Android

- **Create Gmail:** If not already, create a Gmail account ([Create here](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/56256?hl=pt-BR). Access Google Play Console to register the account as an organization. [More details available here](https://developer.android.com/distribute/console?hl=pt-br).

#### For Apple

- **Apple Developer Program Registration:** To register an account for Apple publication, follow the steps to enroll in the Apple Developer Program as an organization and register legal organizational accounts. ([Apple Enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) and [Organization Registration](link)).
- It's crucial that the organization name in the store reflects the chosen app name.

#### After Production Turnover

- Any adjustments or questions can be sent through our customer service channels or on the Heflo communication portal.
