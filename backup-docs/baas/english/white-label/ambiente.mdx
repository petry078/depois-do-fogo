## Available Platforms

### Internet Banking

BMP's Internet Banking allows clients to perform financial transactions such as TED, PIX, and bill payments via computers or mobile devices. This platform offers convenient and secure access, eliminating the need to visit physical branches.

### Apple and Google Apps

Our apps are available on the main mobile digital distribution platforms — Apple App Store and Google Play Store. They provide all functionalities anywhere, offering a complete banking solution directly on your mobile device.

### Back Office Portal

Developed by BMP, the Back Office Portal is a powerful tool for business management. It enables authorized users to manage accounts and monitor financial results, all on a single digital platform.

#### Who Grants Access to the Back Office Portal?

Access to the back office portal is exclusively controlled by BMP. Release of back office operators occurs only upon formalization in the back office agreement, ensuring security and compliance.

#### Available Functionalities and Profiles

**Consultation Profile**
This profile is ideal for users requiring restricted access, primarily for consultation:

- Dashboard
- Account opening (Movement or Escrow)
- Report extraction
- Billing
- Issuance of vouchers
- Fee registration
- CNAB import and export
- History of changes

**Administrator Profile**
The administrator profile offers full access and control over operations and settings in the digital environment:

- Dashboard
- Account opening (Movement or Escrow)
- Report extraction
- Billing
- Issuance of vouchers
- Fee registration
- CNAB import and export
- Management of digital platforms (Internet Banking/Apps)
- Definition of profiles for operators (consultation/Movement/Account Holder)
- Transaction approval
- History of changes

## Conclusion

With these digital environments, BMP ensures that both operators and end customers enjoy a modern, secure, and highly functional banking experience tailored to their business needs and daily financial operations.
