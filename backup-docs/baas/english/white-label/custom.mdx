We offer a range of customization options so that your app experience reflects your company's visual identity. The available customizations include:

### Company Logo

- **Upload Format**: The logo must be uploaded in **.svg** format to ensure high-quality display in the apps. This practice ensures that the logo is presented with excellence, providing a superior experience for users.

### Colors

- **Primary and Secondary Colors**: It is possible to customize the primary and secondary colors that are vital for applying visual components of the product. Use your company's official colors to maintain visual consistency.
- **Upload Format**: Colors must be provided in hexadecimal code, such as #FFFFFF for white.

## Illustration of some of our portals

- Customize the background of the app's startup screens and receipts issued via app or Internet Banking, ensuring alignment with your brand's aesthetics.
