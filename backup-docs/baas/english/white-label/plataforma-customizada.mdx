**BMP** offers an innovative banking business model through our **White Label** platform, allowing partners from various sectors to use our technology to offer personalized financial solutions. This model, known as "white labeling," enables services developed by one entity to be used by other brands, offering financial, operational, and market advantages.

## Introduction

The White Label platform was created to empower any partner to operate in their business segment using our technology, facilitating the offering of customized financial solutions to their clients. This approach promotes agility in processes, regardless of the business sector, consolidating the concept of "white labeling."

## Concept

The concept behind BMP's **White Label** is to enable your company to effectively and swiftly manage its own bank, while maintaining fully customizable brand identity. BMP provides the necessary technological and regulatory infrastructure in collaboration with Bacen, ensuring that the solution is ready for use under the Banking as a Service (BaaS) model. This allows your clients to perform transfers, reconciliations, payments, receipts, and other banking operations, maintaining the reliability of your business.

## Advantages

We offer a range of financial solutions that directly meet market needs, helping you attract and retain customers through significant benefits:

- Speed in product delivery.
- Access to all banking functionalities without the need for in-house development.
- Total control of your plataform at your fingertips.

- Simplified account opening process.
- Use of Escrow account for secure transactions.
- Efficiency in payment and bill registration.
- Ease of operations via PIX and TED.
- Capability to perform payment splits.
- And many other features designed to enrich the banking experience.

## Conclusion

Through BMP's White Label platform, you not only offer financial services under your brand but also gain a partner committed to excellence in digital banking solutions. We are here to help you expand and enhance your financial business with confidence and innovation.
