## Introduction

After the complete and successful execution of the testing scenario, the transition phase to the production environment begins. This page outlines the critical steps to initiate the productive journey, ensuring a smooth transition and effective monitoring of initial operations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Validation and Signoff">
    The Product Owners (POs) and Quality Assurance (QAs) team validate the tests performed by the partner. After validation, the results are sent to the Commercial Executive to finalize the homologation, marking the beginning of the signoff process and the release of production credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implementation in the Production Environment">
    We execute the procedures previously tested in the homologation environment, such as callbacks, HMAC, and handshake, but now focusing on the production environment. This step involves intense communications via emails and working groups to ensure that all configurations are successfully replicated in the production environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release for Production">
    The operational team releases the use in production, generating the production client ID, which is sent by the Implementation team. This team will also conduct the assisted operation process for the first transactions over a period of 30 days, ensuring that any potential issues are quickly identified and resolved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quality Assurance Monitoring

During the first week of operation, BMP will perform constant and synchronized monitoring of the operations. We recommend that tools be used moderately at first, with gradual growth to ensure stability and adjust any discrepancies without negatively impacting end users.

### Tips for a Smooth Transition

- **Constant Monitoring**: Keep the technical support team alert for any signs of unexpected issues.
- **Effective Communication**: Ensure that all parties involved are constantly informed about progress and any challenges encountered.
- **Rapid Feedback**: Encourage immediate feedback on operations to quickly adjust processes if necessary.

By following these steps and maintaining rigorous monitoring, the transition to production will be effective, and the systems will be ready to operate fully to meet the needs of end users.
