This introduction is designed to ensure that all aspects of the system are functional and secure before entering the production environment. The following steps must be completed to ensure the integrity and operability of BMP solutions.

<Warning>

This checklist is mandatory for entry into production.

</Warning>

<Steps>

<Step title="Comunication Channel">

You as our partner will be added to a communication channel to facilitate interaction between everyone involved in the project.

</Step>

<Step title="Project Scope">

A project scope alignment will be carried out and the schedule will be presented.

<Info>

At this stage, count on our team to present our APIs and our cases for a better definition of your project.

</Info>

</Step>

<Step title="Public Key Registration">

In this step, the public key will be registered.

</Step>

<Step title="Delivery of Homologation Credentials">

At this stage, our team will provide you with the credentials for the homologation environment.

</Step>

<Step title="JWT and Token Creation">

Implement JWTs and access tokens for authentication and authorization.

</Step>

<Step title="External Development">

The partner must perform the integration with our APIs.

</Step>

<Step title="BMP Internal Homologation">

Perform internal approval tests to validate integrations and functionalities.

</Step>

<Step title="Signoff">

Obtain final approval from all BackOffice areas involved.

</Step>

<Step title="Production Accreditation">

Perform the necessary procedures to prepare and approve the system for production.

</Step>

<Step title="Delivery of the Production Environment">

Transferir o sistema para o ambiente produtivo e iniciar as operações.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Planning 

* Communication will be done through the project group via WhatsApp;
* Our opening hours are from 9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday;
* We do not issue Production Credentials on Fridays and the day before holidays;
* Practice 1 request per second (Production Rate Limit).

## Conclusion

By completing all the steps in this checklist, the responsible team can be sure that the system is ready to be launched in the production environment, providing a safe and efficient experience for end users.
